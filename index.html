01
Computer vision / Uncertainty

When a prediction needs a little perspective.

Comparing a Probabilistic U-Net and MC Dropout for skin lesion segmentation on ISIC 2018. The Probabilistic U-Net achieved 0.879 Dice versus 0.860, while uncertainty maps revealed where global calibration metrics missed ambiguous boundaries.

PyTorch · SLURM · HPC · 2,594 images

02
Language models / Evaluation

Translation that learns from a second opinion.

A translate, cross-review, and correct pipeline using Mistral and Qwen for English-to-German survey translation. Precise error feedback improved trap avoidance from 39% / 51% to 83% / 88% across the two models.

Mistral-7B · Qwen2.5-7B · COMET · TRAPD

MLOps / Deployment

From a trained model to a working application.

An end-to-end food segmentation pipeline covering 104 classes, versioned experiments, containerised inference, and automated CI/CD. Served through FastAPI and BentoML on Google Cloud Run, with approximately 100 ms inference per image.
